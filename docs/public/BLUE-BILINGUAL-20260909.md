# H3 blue identity and bilingual launch experience

2026-09-09. Implemented in the local website; no live-domain deployment.

The design uses dark blue and cool neutrals with a gold H3 signature. This is an editorial choice for a composed engineering identity, not a measured claim that a particular color guarantees customer trust. Paperozi replaces the previous Latin and Korean font families, including interface labels and canvas lettering. All nine weights use the exact jsDelivr font URLs supplied by the owner, with `font-display: swap` and system fallbacks. The fonts are loaded from the CDN on demand; the website does not claim offline font availability. [Font information](https://noonnu.cc/font_page/1456).

The NVIDIA concept adds a populated board, memory modules, radiator fins, cooling tubes, power cables, fasteners, titanium-colored framing, a ribbed navy facade and smoked glass. It is a design visualization, not a validated physical assembly or a promise of the depicted GPU count.

The Mac family uses brighter neutral silver materials and white studio lighting, with revised front connections and proportions. Sources: [Mac mini](https://www.apple.com/mac-mini/), [Mac Studio](https://www.apple.com/mac-studio/), and [existing Mac Pro 2023](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111343). Mac Pro remains an existing-equipment integration package. H3 badge placement is a package concept, not an Apple partnership or a modification already delivered to customers. Apple reference images were inspected privately; they are not redistributed as website artwork.

## Language behavior

KO / EN controls the entire page, consultation form, showcase and robotics demonstration through a reviewed English dictionary. The URL parameter `lang=ko` or `lang=en` takes priority; the choice persists in local storage where available. Changing language reloads the page and selects the corresponding film, narration, captions and poster. There is no machine translation request at runtime. An unsaved consultation draft is not retained across a language reload.

## Film delivery

Two 30-second Full HD films use shared procedural product geometry, localized typography, Gemini 2.5 Pro Preview TTS / Charon narration and an original synthesized score. No transition swooshes are added. Voice generation is disclosed beside the player. The Korean narration was shortened and regenerated after an external audio review flagged a possible missing first syllable in NVIDIA and rushed articulation. The English recording uses a 1.04× pitch-preserving timing adjustment. The Korean closing H3 signature is positioned in the final brand shot; original takes are retained privately.

The Mac Pro segment receives a targeted geometry correction at 18–22 seconds. Final videos are decoded and inspected from the assembled exports, rather than relying solely on render completion. External AI audio review is not human approval, and sampled stills are not a claim of full audiovisual human review.
